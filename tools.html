Neuroanatomy · Cell Mapping

NeuroFlow

A complete, browser-based pipeline for mapping labeled cells in histological brain sections onto a reference atlas — from raw images to region-by-region cell counts, all in one window.

Launch NeuroFlow Runs 100% locally — your images stay on your device
Developed by Aarushi Rao, WSURP Undergraduate Researcher. See the team page →

What it does

NeuroFlow guides you through the full workflow of quantifying fluorescently labeled cells across the mouse brain. Import your section images, align them to the Allen Brain Atlas, detect labeled cells, and get a clean, exportable table of cell counts for every brain region — no coding required.

How it works — five simple steps

1

Cropper

Import your section images (PNG / JPG / WebP / BMP) and crop them to the tissue.

2

Arranger

Order your sections along the rostral–caudal axis of the brain.

3

Scaler

Register each section to its matching coronal atlas plate to assign brain regions.

4

Quantifier

Detect labeled cells and place them onto the registered atlas coordinates.

5

Analyzer

Review per-region cell counts on a 3D brain and export the results to Excel.

Highlights

Allen Brain Atlas registration Interactive 3D brain viewer One-click Excel export Auto-saves your progress Works fully offline Private — nothing is uploaded

Quick start

Click Launch NeuroFlow, then drag your first section image into the Cropper. Follow the tabs left to right — Cropper → Arranger → Scaler → Quantifier → Analyzer — and export your region counts at the end. The tool remembers your work between steps.

Electrophysiology · Surgery Planning

Neuropixels Planner

An interactive 3D tool for planning Neuropixels probe trajectories on the Allen Mouse Brain atlas. Aim your probe, read out bregma-relative coordinates and arm angles, and see every brain region the track passes through — before you ever pick up a drill.

Launch Neuropixels Planner Runs 100% locally — no install, no server
Developed by Dr. Guang-Wei Zhang, Principal Investigator. See the team page →

What it does

Planning a Neuropixels insertion means choosing where the probe enters the brain, what angle to set on the stereotaxic arm, and how deep to go — while making sure the track hits your target and avoids the wrong structures. The Neuropixels Planner lets you do all of this visually on a 3D mouse brain built from the Allen CCFv3 atlas, with a live-brain (Qiu 2018) coordinate correction, and hands you the exact numbers to dial into your rig.

How to use it

1

Set the entry

Click the brain surface to drop the probe entry point, or drag the cyan ball to slide it.

2

Aim & set depth

Drag the orange tip to aim the probe and set insertion depth in one motion.

3

Read the numbers

Get entry & target AP/ML/DV, ML/AP tilt, and a plain-language "set the arm" instruction.

4

Check the track

Review the ordered list of brain regions the probe crosses, with depth spans.

Highlights

Interactive 3D CCFv3 brain 316 Allen summary structures Bregma-relative coordinates Qiu 2018 live-brain & CCF stereotaxic frames Search & pin any region Works fully offline

Quick start

Click Launch Neuropixels Planner. Orbit with a drag, zoom with the wheel, then click the brain to drop your entry point and drag the orange tip to aim. The left panel updates live with the coordinates and arm angles to set on your stereotaxic rig. Numbers are planning estimates — always cross-check against your stereotaxic atlas.

Behavior · Video Annotation

Social Behavior Annotator

A browser-based GUI for efficiently annotating social behaviors in mouse video recordings — frame by frame, with custom behavior labels — built to power our Animal Behavioral Dataset effort.

Launch Annotator (v1.2) Runs 100% locally — your videos stay on your device

What it does

Turning raw behavior videos into labeled data is slow and tedious. The Social Behavior Annotator streamlines it: load a video, step through it frame by frame, mark when each behavior starts and stops, and export clean annotations ready for analysis or model training. It runs entirely in your browser — no installation, and your videos never leave your computer.

Highlights

Runs entirely in the browser Frame-by-frame keyboard navigation Custom behavior labels Multi-animal annotation Standard export formats Private — nothing is uploaded

Quick start

Click Launch Annotator, load a video, and use the keyboard shortcuts to step through frames and mark behavior segments. We welcome feedback, feature ideas, and contributors — background in web development, computer vision, or behavioral neuroscience is especially welcome. Reach out at guangwei.zhang@vcuhealth.org.
