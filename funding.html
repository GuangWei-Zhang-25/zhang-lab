Funding Sources

Foundations, federal institutes, and university programs whose support makes our research possible.

Whitehall Foundation
Research Grant
Whitehall Foundation

Supports our study of the circuit architecture underlying the control of naturalistic parenting behaviors in rodent models. The Foundation funds basic research in vertebrate and invertebrate neurobiology, with a focus on neural mechanisms that underlie behavior.

Dr. Guang-Wei Zhang, Principal Investigator
whitehall.org
NIH
National Institute on Aging
R24 Pilot Grant
National Institute on Aging, NIH

Supports our work on group social behaviors in aging, extending our behavioral and circuit approaches from pairs of animals to social groups across the lifespan.

Dr. Guang-Wei Zhang, Principal Investigator
nia.nih.gov
Alzheimer's Association
AARF Research Fellowship
Alzheimer's Association

Supports our study of the subcortical deficits underlying social deficits in Alzheimer's disease, connecting circuit-level dysfunction to the loss of social engagement seen in patients.

alz.org
Brain & Behavior Research Foundation
Young Investigator Grant
Brain & Behavior Research Foundation

Supports research on estrogen-related female neuropsychiatric disorders. The BBRF Young Investigator program backs outstanding early-career scientists in neuroscience and psychiatry.

Dr. Can Tao, Awardee
bbrfoundation.org
VCU Office of the Provost
Convergence Team Planning Grant
VCU Office of the Provost

Supports the launch of a cross-disciplinary team bridging neuroscience, engineering, and neural circuit research, bringing together expertise that none of the three labs holds alone.

Led by Dr. Guang-Wei Zhang, in collaboration with Dr. Priyodarshan Goswamee (Department of Neuroscience and Anatomy) and Dr. Dean Krusienski (VCU School of Engineering).
provost.vcu.edu
