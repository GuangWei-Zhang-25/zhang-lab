Principal Investigator

Guang-Wei Zhang, MD, PhD

Principal Investigator

Assistant Professor, Department of Anatomy and Neurobiology
Virginia Commonwealth University

Dr. Zhang's research focuses on understanding the neural mechanisms underlying social behavior and how these mechanisms are disrupted in neuropsychiatric disorders. The laboratory uses cutting-edge techniques including optogenetics, chemogenetics, in vivo imaging, and sophisticated behavioral analysis to investigate neural circuits controlling social impairment, with the ultimate goal of identifying novel therapeutic targets for conditions such as autism, depression, and anxiety disorders.

Research Interests

  • Neural circuits controlling social behavior
  • Prosocial behaviors and empathy
  • Stress-induced anxiety and depression
  • Excitation-inhibition balance
  • Therapeutic target identification

How to Apply

Our lab provides a collaborative and supportive environment with access to state-of-the-art facilities and techniques for scientific discovery. We are committed to mentoring the next generation of neuroscientists.

What We're Looking For

Postdoctoral Fellows

PhD in neuroscience or related field. Experience with optogenetics, electrophysiology, or in vivo imaging is highly valued.

Graduate Students

Strong academic record and genuine interest in neural circuit research. Apply through VCU's Neuroscience or Integrative Life Sciences programs.

Research Technicians

Bachelor's degree in biology or related field. Prior research experience preferred but not required. Must be detail-oriented and reliable.

We are committed to fostering an inclusive and diverse research environment. We encourage applications from individuals of all backgrounds and believe that diversity strengthens our science.

To Apply: Please send your CV, a cover letter describing your research interests and career goals, and contact information for three references to Dr. Zhang.
