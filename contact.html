Visiting Our Lab

By Car

From I-95/I-64:

  • Take Exit 74C (Broad Street)
  • Turn right onto N 10th Street
  • Turn left onto E Marshall Street
  • Sanger Hall on your right

Parking: Visitor parking in D Deck or A Deck

By Air

Richmond International Airport (RIC) is 15 minutes from campus.

  • Taxi/Uber/Lyft available
  • Rental cars available
  • Airport shuttle to downtown

Public Transit

GRTC Transit serves VCU Medical Campus:

  • Multiple bus routes stop nearby
  • Pulse BRT connects campuses
  • Check GRTC for schedules
