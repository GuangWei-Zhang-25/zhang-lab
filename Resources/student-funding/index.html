Individual awards students can apply for directly, with a faculty sponsor, to fund their own training and research.

NIH Individual Fellowships (F30 / F31 / F32 / F99–K00)

Open information

The Ruth L. Kirschstein NRSA family of individual fellowships. F31 supports predoctoral PhD students in the dissertation-research stage; F30 supports MD/PhD and other dual-degree students; F32 supports postdoctoral fellows; F99/K00 bridges the predoc-to-postdoc transition. Awards may cover stipend, institutional allowance, tuition and fees, and childcare costs.

Conference travel awards

Support for presenting your work at meetings, both from the Society for Neuroscience and from VCU.

SfN Trainee Professional Development Award

Application

Travel award for undergraduates, graduate students, post-baccalaureate scholars, and postdocs presenting at the SfN annual meeting: $1,000 for trainees in the U.S., Canada, or Mexico ($2,500 internationally) plus complimentary registration. Requires an approved abstract, current SfN membership, and a short essay; recipients present in a special TPDA poster session and attend two professional-development workshops.

VCU Graduate School Travel Grants

VCU students

Up to $500 toward conference registration, travel, and lodging for degree-seeking master's and doctoral students who are presenting their work. Maximum of two trips per academic year; issued as a departmental reimbursement. First-professional students (D.D.S., M.D., Pharm.D.) are not eligible.
