Registration

Two-day intensive from Columbia's SHARP program at the Mailman School of Public Health, covering negotiation, staffing, leadership, mentoring, people management, time management, project management, and networking. Aimed at senior postdocs, associate research scientists preparing for independence, and new faculty.
