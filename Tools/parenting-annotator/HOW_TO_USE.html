How to use the Parenting Annotator

From opening your videos to results and an Excel table.

  1. Open the annotator. Double-click parenting_annotator_jie_V5.html (or open its page on the website) in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Open your videos. Click Open Video Folder, choose the folder with your videos (MP4, MKV or AVI) and allow it to edit files. Next time, use Reopen.
  3. Pick a video from the list on the left.
  4. Skip the beginning (optional). In Analysis range, type the start time, e.g. 2:00. Click apply to all videos to skip the same minutes in every video.
  5. Label a behavior. Click a behavior at the bottom (or press 1–9), press I where it starts and O where it ends.
  6. Fix labels. Drag a bar's edge to change its start or end, drag it to another row to change the behavior, double-click it to edit or delete. Ctrl+Z undoes.
  7. Mark what you checked. Press V at the start and V again at the end of the part you have fully reviewed.
  8. Change behaviors (optional). Click Edit categories at the bottom (or double-click a behavior) to add, rename or remove behaviors.
  9. Saving is automatic. The badge at the top right shows Saved.
  10. See the results. Click Results at the top: totals, frequency and bout length per behavior, an ethogram for every video, and a table. Choose the measure at the top right; click a bout to jump to it.
  11. Export results. Click Export CSV (Results tab). The tables appear in the exports folder inside your video folder and open in Excel.
Good to know: your videos never leave your computer. Press ? in the annotator for all shortcuts. In Safari or Firefox, drag your videos together with their .json files onto the page and save with Download JSON.
