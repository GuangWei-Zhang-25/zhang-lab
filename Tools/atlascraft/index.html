AtlasCraft

An AI-agent and human cooperative system for crafting anatomical atlases. Turn curated 2D label maps into aligned, labelled 3D atlases, explore regional boundaries, and keep a record of the reconstruction.

Developed by Can Tao & Guang-Wei Zhang.

AtlasCraft was used to develop our 3D Mouse Spinal Cord Atlas.

The demo opens directly in your browser. To build an atlas from your own sections, download the package and start the local app below. Reconstruction runs on your computer and needs no API key.

Launch on your computer

  1. Download and extract the complete ZIP. Keep the files together in the extracted AtlasCraft folder. If you already have this folder locally, use its launch file.
  2. Use Python 3.10 or newer. Install it if needed before the first launch.
  3. Open the launch file. On Mac, double-click Launch_AtlasCraft.command; on Windows, double-click Launch_AtlasCraft.bat. The first launch installs dependencies and needs internet access. Later launches reuse the installed environment.
  4. Keep the terminal window open. AtlasCraft opens at http://127.0.0.1:8766. Choose Try synthetic example, or upload your curated JSON/ZIP input to build your own atlas.

Prepare your sections

Supply human-reviewed categorical label maps, regional IDs, and section positions. The package includes synthetic examples and a guide to input preparation. Inspect the resulting atlas before accepting the reconstructed anatomy.
