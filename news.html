August 2026

R24 Pilot Grant to Study Group Social Behavior in Aging

The Zhang Lab has been awarded an NIH NIA R24 Pilot & Feasibility Grant from the Animal Models for the Social Dimensions of Health and Aging Research Network to study naturalistic group social behavior in aging and social loss. This award will enable us to explore how social dynamics change across the lifespan and in response to social loss, using rodent models. We are grateful for this support to advance our understanding of the social dimensions of aging.

August 2026

Dr. Zhang Awarded a Whitehall Foundation Research Grant

Dr. Guang-Wei Zhang has been awarded a Whitehall Foundation Research Grant to study the circuit architecture underlying the control of naturalistic parenting behaviors using rodent models. The Whitehall Foundation supports basic research in vertebrate and invertebrate neurobiology, with a focus on neural mechanisms that underlie behavior. We are grateful for the generous support from the Whitehall Foundation to the Zhang Lab.

August 3–6, 2026

Dr. Zhang Named a 2026 NIA Butler-Williams Scholar

Dr. Guang-Wei Zhang was named a 2026 Butler-Williams Scholar by the National Institute on Aging (NIA) at the National Institutes of Health (NIH), and attended the program at the NIH campus on August 3–6, 2026. The program is named in honor of Robert N. Butler, MD, the founding director of the NIA, and T. Franklin Williams, MD, its second director. Now in its fourth decade, it brings early-career investigators new to aging research together with NIA leadership and program staff for an intensive week on the science, gaps, and opportunities in the field. Congratulations!
