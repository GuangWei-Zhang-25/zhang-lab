Lab News

Updates, events, and highlights from the Zhang Lab.

Jan 2027 – Jan 2029

Dr. Can Tao Receives BBRF Young Investigator Award

Dr. Can Tao (Catherine) has been awarded a Brain & Behavior Research Foundation (BBRF) Young Investigator Grant, running from January 2027 through January 2029, to study estrogen-related female neuropsychiatric disorders. The BBRF Young Investigator program supports early-career scientists with up to $70,000 over two years to generate pilot data and launch independent research programs in neuroscience and psychiatry. Congratulations, Catherine, on this prestigious award!

July 7–13, 2026

Zhang Lab Selected for Anthropic's "Built with Claude: Life Sciences" Hackathon

The Zhang Lab has been selected to participate in Built with Claude: Life Sciences, a global virtual hackathon hosted by Anthropic & Cerebral Valley in partnership with the Gladstone Institutes (July 7–13, 2026). Just 500 participants were chosen worldwide to build with Claude Science and Claude Code across a Research track and a Build track, competing for $100k in credits. Excited to see what we can build in one week!

Zhang Lab Receives VCU Convergence Seed Award

The Zhang Lab has been awarded a VCU Convergence Seed Award, supporting a collaborative project with Dr. Dean Krusienski (VCU School of Engineering) and Dr. Priyodarshan Goswamee (Department of Neuroscience and Anatomy). Dr. Zhang Selected for the 2026 NIA Butler-Williams Scholars Program

Dr. Guang-Wei Zhang has been awarded a place in the 2026 Butler-Williams Scholars (BWS) Program, sponsored by the National Institute on Aging (NIA) at the National Institutes of Health (NIH). The program will be held August 3–6, 2026, bringing together a distinguished group of scientists for information sharing and network building — a career advancement tradition that has continued for over 30 years. Congratulations!
